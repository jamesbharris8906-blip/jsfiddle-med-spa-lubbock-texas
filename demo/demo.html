PicoSure Pro

PicoSure Pro uses very fast pulses of light to help with dark spots, sun damage, acne scars, and uneven color. The pulses are so quick that the skin gets help without a lot of heat, which means less time healing for many people. You may feel a snap or a warm tingle, and the skin may look pink for a short time. Over a few visits, spots often fade and skin looks clearer. It can also help with tattoo removal for some inks. We will look at your skin tone, your spots, and your history before we pick a setting, because safe care starts with a good match. If you have spent years in the Lubbock sun, this can be a strong choice.

Skin Boosters

Skin Boosters are small injections that feed the skin with moisture from the inside. They are not fillers, and they do not change the shape of your face. They help skin look dewy, soft, and bright, like the glass skin look so many people love from Korean beauty care. They work well on the face, neck, and hands, and they are a good choice if your skin feels dry, dull, or tired even when you use good creams. The visit is quick, and most people have tiny bumps for a day that fade fast. Over a few weeks, skin often feels bouncier and looks more alive. It is a quiet treatment, but the change shows in photos and in the mirror.

Botox

Botox is a short visit with a clear job. It softens lines made by muscle movement, like lines on the forehead, between the brows, and around the eyes. It works by easing those small muscles, so the lines they make get softer. The pinches are quick, and most people go back to work or class right after. You will see the change build over a few days, and it can last for months. We talk first about how much softening you want. Some people want the lines mostly gone. Others want just a little softening, so they still move and show feeling. We start with your wish, and we write down what we did, so the next visit can match what you liked.

LaseMD Ultra

LaseMD Ultra is a gentle laser that helps dull skin, sun spots, and fine lines look brighter. The Lubbock sun can leave brown spots and a tired tone, even if you wear sunscreen now. This laser works on the top layers of skin, helping fresh, bright skin come forward. Most people feel warmth, and their skin looks pink for a short time, then it settles. There is little rest time after, which is why many people pick it before a wedding, a reunion, or a family photo. We pair it with sun care, because bright new skin needs care in strong sun. If you have never had a laser, this is often a kind first step.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers add soft volume where age, weight change, or just time has taken it away. They can lift cheeks, soften smile lines, shape lips, and smooth the lines around the mouth. The key is balance. A little in the right place can make you look rested. Too much in the wrong place can look odd, so we go slow and check as we go. We talk about what kind of filler fits each spot, how long it tends to last, and what the visit feels like. Most people have some swelling or a small bruise for a few days, and that is normal. We also talk about what you want to keep. If you love your smile, we keep your smile. Fillers should fit your face, not change who you are.

Titanium Laser Hair Removal

Titanium Laser Hair Removal helps cut down on shaving, waxing, and ingrown hairs. The laser targets hair at the root, and with a series of visits, many people see far less hair and smoother skin. It works on legs, underarms, the bikini line, the face, the back, and other spots. The device has cooling built in, so the visit feels more comfortable than older lasers for many people. You will need more than one visit because hair grows in cycles, and we treat the hair that is ready each time. We will tell you how to get ready, what to skip before your visit, and how to care for your skin after. For busy people in Lubbock, fewer shaving days is a small gift that adds up fast.

Keralase

Keralase is for hair that feels thin, flat, or slow to grow. It pairs a gentle laser with a serum that helps the scalp and hair follicles. Many people first notice more shedding than they like, or a part that looks wider, or hair that will not hold its shape. Keralase aims to help the scalp get healthier so hair can grow thicker and stronger. It is not a quick fix. It takes a series of visits and time, and we talk about that up front. We also talk about hair care, stress, food, and health, because hair tells a story about the whole body. Potenza RF Microneedling

Potenza RF Microneedling pairs tiny needles with radiofrequency energy to help your skin make new collagen. It can help with fine lines, loose skin, acne scars, and rough texture. Most people feel a warm, prickly feeling, and their skin may look pink for a day or two, like a light sunburn. The change is slow and real. Over a few weeks, skin often feels firmer and smoother. We talk through how many visits make sense for your skin, what to expect after each one, and how to care for your skin while it heals. XERF

XERF is a newer way to help skin look firmer without needles or a long rest. It uses focused energy under the skin to help collagen grow, and it can help the jawline, cheeks, neck, and brow look firmer over time. People who do not want needles often ask about XERF first. The visit feels warm, and most people go back to normal life the same day. The change builds week by week as your skin makes new collagen. That slow build is why it looks natural. Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first if my skin looks tired after summer?

Start with a talk. We will look at your skin, ask about sun, sleep, and care at home, and suggest a first step, like a gentle laser, moisture care, or a simple new routine.

How do I know if Botox or fillers are better for me?

Botox relaxes lines made by muscle movement, while fillers add volume where it has been lost. At your visit, we will look at your face, listen to your goal, and suggest the one that fits best, or a mix of both.

Do laser treatments hurt, and how long do I need to rest after?

Most people feel warmth, a snap, or a light tingle. Many laser visits have little downtime, with pink skin for a day or two. We will tell you what to expect for the exact treatment you pick.

How many visits will I need to see a change?

It depends on the treatment and your skin. Some changes show in days, while collagen and hair treatments build over weeks. We will give you a clear plan with a likely number of visits.

Can I get treatment if I have sensitive skin?

Yes, in many cases. Tell us about your skin, allergies, and past reactions. We can pick gentler options, test a small area, and adjust the plan to keep your skin calm and safe.

What does Korean beauty-inspired care mean at K-Glow MD Aesthetics?

It means a focus on bright, healthy, hydrated skin and natural results. We use small, steady steps, good daily care, and treatments that help your skin glow without looking overdone.
